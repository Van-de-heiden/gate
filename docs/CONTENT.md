# Content and media

Gate's German explanations, scenarios, diagrams and quiz questions are original, AI-assisted
editorial material under the repository's MIT licence. They link to further reading in the
curriculum; they are not copied textbook chapters and are not authored or endorsed by those
organisations. Independent subject-matter review is still needed before a production release.

The same historical photo is deliberately read in two different ways: as a single observation
in time, and as a source with context and metadata. It is loaded only when the learner requests
it. The current edition has **one source photograph**, not a complete offline image library.

## Editorial standard

Each lesson has stable IDs, one explicit objective, at least two teaching cards, a visual whose
labels/units match the text, an optional reference photo, an active-recall reflection, a takeaway
and four explained questions. New variants must test taught concepts and use plausible distractors.
Source links are further-reading pointers, not claims of external review.

The repetition intervals and question counts are product heuristics, not a validated cognitive
diagnosis or a promise of a particular learning outcome. Completed chapters and spaced question
success are different metrics. Economic examples are conceptual teaching, not investment advice.
